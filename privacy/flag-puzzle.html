앱은 무료 제공을 위해 Google AdMob 광고를 표시합니다. 앱은 어린이 대상 앱이므로 모든 광고를 아동 대상 처리(child-directed)로 요청하며, 맞춤형(관심 기반) 광고와 리마케팅을 사용하지 않고, 전체이용가 수준의 광고만 요청합니다. 앱은 광고 목적으로 Android 광고 ID 등 어린이의 영구 식별자를 수집하거나 전송하지 않습니다. 다만 광고를 전달·측정하고 부정행위를 막기 위해 Google이 IP 주소, 기기 및 운영체제 종류 등 제한된 기술 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다. 보상형 광고는 이용자가 "국기보기" 버튼을 직접 누른 경우에만 표시됩니다. 자세한 내용은 Google 개인정보처리방침(https://policies.google.com/privacy)을 참고하세요.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-15

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how "국기 퍼즐 세계여행 - 어린이 국기 (Flag Puzzle World Trip - Kids)" (package name com.godzza.flag_puzzle_adventure, the "App"), published on Google Play by the developer Team Bylee ("we" or the "Developer"), handles information. The App is an educational game for children and families and is designed to handle as little information as possible.

2. Information we collect

The App has no sign-up and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts or precise location. Game progress (collected flags, score, settings and language) is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob so it can be offered for free. Because the App is directed at children, all ad requests are tagged as child-directed, personalized (interest-based) ads and remarketing are disabled, and only ads suitable for general audiences are requested. The App does not collect or transmit the Android Advertising ID or any other persistent identifier of children for advertising purposes. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as IP address and device and operating-system type. Rewarded ads are shown only when the user chooses to tap the "See flag" button. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

4. Children's privacy

The App complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

5. Device permissions

The App only uses the Internet permission (to load ads) and the vibration permission (for game feedback). It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage.

6. Voice feature

The answer-voice (text-to-speech) feature uses the speech engine built into your device. Your voice is never recorded or transmitted.

7. Retention and deletion

Because game progress is stored only on your device, uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings permanently deletes it.

8. Open source and credits

Flag images: flag-icons (MIT License). World map: Simple World Map by Al MacDonald, edited by Fritz Lekschas (CC BY-SA 3.0). Landmark illustrations are original artwork made for the App.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
